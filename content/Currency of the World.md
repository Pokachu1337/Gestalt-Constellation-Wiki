The most well known denominations of wealth are coins of copper, silver and gold. The golden coin is the defacto standard for accounting all around [[Ort]]. Some civilizations have also minted coins from other metals, most prominently the platinum coins of the [[The Precursors|precursors]] and the [[Sahra|dragons kingdom]]. However counting large sums of coins proves difficult, so wealth also comes in other standardized forms. Most commonly seen by the rich are the standard imperial five-pound silverbars printed with an imperial seal to proof that they indeed contain silver of the right purity and weight. Some merchants that hail from the desert also trade in two and five-pound gold bars, minted by the [[Kharagon|dragon]] herself. Of course we cannot forget jewelry and art, holding value not (only) in metal, but also their craftsmanship. For example some travelers wear silver armbands they use to pay for the stops along their journey. And of course we cannot forget gems. For trades between nobles, churches and the major powers. Handing over a few gems of lesser value as upfront payment is sometimes done as a symbolic gesture when a deal of immense value is finalized.
