A Knight of the [[The Enlightened of Mutter Mond|Enlightened]], a merciless Paladin to the pale light of [[Mutter Mond]]. 
### What is a potential thing that others might have heard about you? 
The knight is a figure rarely seen alone. As a knight sworn to Mutter Mond, they always travels with the High Priestess Anne-Laure of Chastain. The knight does not give his own name, but that of his patron goddess, Lunéciel Mondkind. They may not carry a weapon, but that does not mean they are unarmed. _(thinking about it, this may not be that uncommon for magic knights)_
### What is the goal of your character in 1-2 Sentences so we know what to expect to dm for you?
Sent out by the Enlightened, they travel with the High Priestess to find the [[Crown of the Waning Moon]], an artifact said to save the world from certain doom, or at least that's what the High Priestesses say.
### Holy commandments of knighthood: 
- The will of the Mondkind is absolute to her Knight, second only to the Mutter Mond herself. 
- ⚠️ The Mondkind will speak. The Knight is a mere vessel it is unfitting for it to speak, especially its own mind. 
- Protecting other Enlightened comes before your safety and before your emotions. 
- You shall honor requests made of you, especially if the come from Mutter's priestesses. 
- You shall be ready for battle at any moment, our enemies are vicious and never rest. 
- Be proud of your faith and your blessed birth. 
- Kill our enemies without mercy, for they have never shown us any. 
- There are more, but this is what I have till now.
- You shall hide your face during the day, for their vile god can not be allowed to know it. 
- Only eat under the sheltering watch of Mutter. 
- Drink only in shadow, where the tainted light can not reach you. 
- ⚠️ means the commandment was broken at least once. 
- ❎ means it is being ignored.
