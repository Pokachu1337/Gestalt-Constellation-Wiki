The towering structure the Thri-kreen call home, known as the Nest, is a massive mound of sand and earth surrounded by stone walls and towers made to blend into the surrounding sands located in the desert to the south of [[Schwert]]. Any travelers that venture too close are quickly spotted and questioned before they even get in sight of the main hive of activity. Very few other than the Thri-kreen themselves have been up and into the Nest itself, its narrow passageways and various rooms hold much for anyone brave enough to assault the Nest, and much like the ants they look like, they will fight ferociously to defend the Nest, especially the Queen held in the center of it all, the ruler of all the Thri-kreen, and the one who birthed them all. Within the Nest are a plethora of rooms each with a purpose, one could be a storage room, or a room used to smith arms for the warriors, or rooms to house pets and other domesticated animals, or libraries of knowledge where instead of paper, they use clay tablets, or even vast rooms to test magical spells or research new spells. All has its place in the Nest. Occasionally a Thri-kreen is ordered to leave the Nest to learn from the outside world, on the basis that information gained would be useful for the Queen and other Thri-kreen leaders. The leaders are the heads of various jobs within the Nest; Forgemaster, the Lead hunter/herdmeister, and the Headmaster of the nursery just to name a few. They meet to discuss the happenings within the have and distribute resources as needed. All are needed to grow the Nest. All must work to better the Nest!
