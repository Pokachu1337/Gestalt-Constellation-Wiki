An ancient [[Precursor Structures|Precursor structure]], that moves through [[Schwert]] by arcane means. Its outside has never fully been seen, the weather simply does not permit, wherever the tower goes. No one has yet seen the roof of the tower. An edict almost as old as the empire itself permits the use of extraordinary resources to investigate the tower. Despite these countless expeditions into the tower, the empire keeps all information they gain close to themselves. What is common knowledge, is that the tower is dangerous and its layout is never the same. The motives for why ancients build the tower are often discussed. The leading theory, going undisputed by the empire of Schwert, is that it is an ancient mage showing off their mastery over illusion magics. The empire only rarely permits those outside its ranks to enter the tower. Most often, if otherwise no expedition could be made into the tower. Legends found in other precursor vaults suggest that at the top of the tower are the instructions for a spell that can reform landscapes, turning mountains into rivers and castles into plains. One should not imagine what an imperial warmage would do with such magic.
