Far in the northern reaches of [[Ort]], where the mountains claw into the sky and snow never fully melts, stands the First Spire, the first known [[Precursor Structures|megastructure]] of the Precursors ever discovered. The structure appears as a perfectly smooth pillar of dark stone rising impossibly high into the clouds, without seam, window, or visible entrance. Around its base lie massive broken rings and half-buried ruins, suggesting the Spire was once part of something even larger. Scholars, treasure hunters, and armies have all tried to enter it, yet only a single doorway has ever been found, a vast arch that leads into darkness deeper than any torch can illuminate. The Spire remains largely unexplored, and many who entered never returned. At the base of the mountain the town of [[Kreiswacht]] lays, its economy based around the explorers the Spire pulls in, selling equipment and proper rest for those who wish to explore the structure at their own risk.
