The shifting dunes are a part of the [[Sahra]] desert known to be impossible to cross. It is considered one of the harshest terrains on the entire continent. The shifting dunes have been plagued by a neverending sandstorm since the beginning of time. There is next to no information on what is actually in the dunes or where the storm comes from, the only thing known is that the dunes move as if waves crossing the ocean. They move unpredictably and in unknown intervals. The innerworkings of the shifting dunes is one of the biggest mysteries of the continent next to the [[Precursor Structures|precursor structures]] and seemingly only [[Kharagon]] herself knows what they hide.
