In the south lies the great desert Sahra, a land of endless dunes, buried cities, and burning winds. It is ruled by the ancient dragon [[Kharagon]], who proclaimed herself God-King thousands of years ago. From her golden capital [[Solkar]], she rules not as a tyrant, but as an eternal monarch, worshiped as a living god by the people of the desert. Neither [[Schwert]] nor [[Krone]] dare march into Sahra, for many armies have vanished beneath its sands, and all know that the Dragon King does not forgive trespassers. Do not go where the dragon rules for their colors change like the season and so does her whim.
