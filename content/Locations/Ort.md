The largest continent of [[Kreis]], mostly claimed by two powerful Factions. The Powerful Empire [[Schwert]] of the East and the Kingdom [[Krone]] of the West. Both dominions were at a long lived war until their current rulers, [[Emperor Alexander]] and [[King Ludwig]] had made a pact to not shed further blood. To call this a peace treaty however is bold, as their relations now are still shaky and some in power do their best to rekindle the war from the shadows. In the north, the great [[Tundra]] lies, a land of cold and mountains, where snow never fully melts, while in the south the desert nation of [[Sahra]] lies.
