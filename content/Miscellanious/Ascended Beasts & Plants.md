It is said that when a beast or plant becomes particularly aged, or happens upon some very particular circumstance (living in an area of wild magic, consuming flesh from a particularly magical/powerful being, extreme trauma, etc.), that their minds may uplift themselves to human intelligence, and they may develop strange powers. Legends speak of beasts centuries old, who learn to assume human form and live hidden among mankind, some for good and some for ill. Still others whisper of those who seclude themselves in the deepest wilderness and hone their minds and bodies until they reach a state of enlightenment.
