Pilgrims flood the main street as the morning sun paints the mountains gold. They have almost reached their goal, [[King Karl Cathedral of Saint Jakob]]. Build over 300 years ago, it is one of the important pilgrimage sites for the followers of [[Vater Sonne]]. Now that the war is taking a break, pilgrims from [[Schwert]] can once again make the journey. The legend goes that long ago along these mountains [[Saint Jakob the Kind]] did his good deeds and that, when it was his time to meet the Vater, his funeral was held in this villages small chapel and his ashes were scattered over the fields with the wind. Later [[King Karl of Krone]] ordered a grand Cathedral be built in honor of the saint. The city lies in three rings ascending up the foothills of the [[Goldschein mountains]]. The up most ring holds the Cathedral and the old town. The middle rings outer border is clearly marked by the city walls. It contains the districts of high town, central market and artisans' hovel. The outer ring is not only the surrounding farms, but also the the simpler parts of town. A low wall, not more then a stone fence, surrounds the formal borders of the city, at the edge of the outer ring. [[Lord Bishop Fromheit von Kirchberg]] rules over the city and serves as the Cathedrals secondary high priest.
