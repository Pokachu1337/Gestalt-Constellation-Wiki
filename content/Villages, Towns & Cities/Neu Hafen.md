The City of Neu Hafen was founded, when Adventurers discovered a Monument of unknown origin that emitted a magical zone all around the [[Island of Einsam]]. This magical zone prohibits anyone inside from speaking lies or dealing unfairly, which has led the city of Neu Hafen to become the central point of trade on the continent. As of now, there are no known instances of people trying to circumvent the spell. Being completely independent from any nation, but important to any and all trade happening on the continent, a sort of truce was established to conserve the peace on the island, which has stood since the cities' founding. In the center of the city, the [[Obelisk of Truth]] still stands for everyone to admire.
