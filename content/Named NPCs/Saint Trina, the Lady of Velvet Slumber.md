Long ago, all the medical knowledge mortalkind had at hand was dominated by healing magics. Healing spells can restore limbs, rebuild lost flesh and more. But users of these magics, historically, were few and far between, and all manner of chronic pains and ailments could not be healed with magic and traditional medicine alone, at the time. Issues of medical import were all too often swept under the rug, and surgery unassisted by all-too-rare healing magics was a death sentence. Thus, great (and preventable) suffering was rife in those days, and is to this very day. Approximately three hundred years ago, the woman who would come to be known as Saint Trina, the Lady of Velvet Slumber, undertook her life's work; the creation of toxins and poisons for medicinal use. A medicine woman who believed in the progress of mortalkind's medicine without reliance on the gods, it was her aim to develop pain-eradicating tinctures and concoctions, and in time, she did exactly that. Her toxins, when distilled from the purple lilies of her homeland and applied correctly, could induce sleep no matter the form or use. When properly applied, her most potent 'eternal sleep' toxins could induce catatonic states for hours at a time. Procedures and surgeries that would normally leave someone worse than when they came in, were now infinitely more survivable. Though her work was censured by the dominant powers at the time, her followers would preserve St. Trina's writings and research, founding the [[House of Sleep]] in the intervening centuries. Today, you can still find carvings and plaques dedicated to St. Trina, depicted as a blindfolded woman in purple, with a raised torch of lilac flame and a sword in her other hand.
