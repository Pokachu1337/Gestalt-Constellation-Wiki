When you dig around in the [[Sahra|desert]] sand for long enough you will sometimes find these strange roots. The size of a carrot, colored a sickly red-yellow. They are wooden and utterly dry. When boiled in water, they become eatable. They taste faint with a hint of sweetness and might be slightly poisonous. To the people the [[Kharagon|Dragon]] claims Her own they taste between bitter and sour, but the poison does not take purchase within them. The Dragon Herself denies any association with these "lesser desert weeds". To the [[The Qamalri|Qamalri]] it is shameful to eat them, as to them they represent gifts from the Dragon they so resent. However they often find themselves to short on food, as to not eat them.
