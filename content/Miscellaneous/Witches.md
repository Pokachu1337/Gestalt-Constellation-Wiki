"Witches are powerful mages as well as devoted caretakers, combining patience, insight, and subtlety with extraordinary willfulness. While other spellcasters might use magic in grand gestures that bend the world to their whims, a witch’s power works in harmony with the people, plants, animals, and magical forces around them. The practice of witchcraft is often rooted in everyday rituals and chores, emphasizing the value in doing things with care and deliberation rather than taking shortcuts." - Marie Léprien
Instead of gaining their powers through ancient bloodlines or pacts with those in power, Witches live in complete harmony with their surroundings, understanding and communicating with it so well that it seems the world works in their favor. Witches can be all sorts of creatures, though exclusively female, they often hold so called "Stations", a responsibility to bear which would turn the world into a darker place if they wouldn't. This rarely causes Witches to live in solitude, however, many of them gathering friends and followers around them, helping those in need but expecting similar help in return. It is said that Witches are the incarnation of consequence. If you are nice to them, they will reward you with a great gift, however be impolite, threaten them or even hurt them and it will come back to haunt you a thousand fold, consequences which are natural law that they can give. Many people are grateful to meet witches, offering food or a place to stay in trade for magical help with whatever ails them. Always call them Miss, always say thank you and always respect the world around you, otherwise you might find yourself turned into a frog for eternity.
