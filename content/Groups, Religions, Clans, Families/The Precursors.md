A civilisation long gone from this world, but their [[Precursor Structures|structures]] still remain, like an echoing whisper. Huge structures below the earth or built into mountains, filled with artifacts, treasures, dangers and magics beyond the imagination. What happened to them is perhaps the greatest secret in history, a point of debate within scientific circles for centuries. All we know is that they were more powerful and further developed than us, which poses the question of what could wipe them away like this.
