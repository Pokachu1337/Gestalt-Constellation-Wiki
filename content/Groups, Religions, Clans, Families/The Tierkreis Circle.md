A druidic order concerned with keeping harmony with nature, through understanding and respect. Observing how nature flows and following or redirecting its path. Only a small portion lives in their monastery in the east of [[Schwert]] where sages lecture anyone willing to listen. The Empire accepts their presence and influence, for as long as they help the villages near them to continue to prosper. Most of their members travel the continent. They welcome the peace as this allows them to more easily travel to the western lands. These travelers travel to observe nature, rediscover their love for it or to follow their own agenda. They show their affiliation through a string colored red, green, orange, blue and brown wrapped five times around something, most commonly the arm or a staff.
