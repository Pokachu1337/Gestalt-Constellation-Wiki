In the northern reaches of [[Krone]], within the [[Blindenhöh]], a region of steep and dangerous mountains, which the High Priestesses seem to ascend and descend with ease, the Enlightened of Mutter Mond are located. The sect of [[Mutter Mond]] is ordered beneath a quiet, unquestioned matriarchy, though few beyond its halls speak of it without unease. Women are taken into priesthood and raised apart from the world, while men are trained as silent, unwavering knights who serve them without hesitation. They preach the gentle grace of the moon and its promise of change, yet their presence unsettles even the faithful, as if something beneath their calm devotion does not fully belong. In the hidden chambers of their mountain temple, the chosen undergo a sacred rite none will describe. Those who emerge return altered, their eyes forever closed, their sight said to come from elsewhere. It is considered wise not to ask what they truly see, nor what may be looking back through them.
