Where do you suppose the dead go, who aren't claimed by any god or faith? Do they wallow in limbo for eternity? What's the point of death if you didn't get much out of life anyway? And if life ain't just a joke -- why are we laughing? The Black Parade is a mystery of a realm, force, faith... scholars cannot agree on its exact nomenclature. What is known is that followers of the Parade, while rare, tend to be warlocks and bards, and maintain that all who die without any "prior commitments", such as devotion to a particular realm or deity, join the Parade -- an endless procession, a macabre marching band, who celebrate life as the dress rehearsal for their great, endless performance in death. Revelling in the concept of being sent to hell, mocking the concept of heaven above; death isn't mourned, but life is celebrated at lavish funerals their followers are known to host -- for it's time for the deceased's great debut, time to join the show. Those who have glimpsed the eternal procession in action report sights macabre and bizarre, of marching undead and souls all dressed in their parade best, their music dark and otherworldly; at once terrifying and marvelous. Who leads the Black Parade? Where is it going? Who walks among these famous living dead? None can say for sure. The only conclusive truth is that their memories will carry on.
