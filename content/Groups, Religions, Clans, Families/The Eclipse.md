The Eclipse is a very scarcely known eldritch cult dating back to the first light, even older than the [[The Precursors|precursors]] themselves. They seek to plunge the world into darkness and eradicate all light. 3 divine beings serve as the gods for this religion. They are known as the [[The Endbringers|Endbringers]]. Firstly the Wolf, that devours the sky, secondly the Snake, that sinks the land and lastly the Fallen Angel, that denies life itself. Their belief says, that when these 3 divine beings are once again reunited, they would eradicate all light and bring an everlasting darkness onto the world. This Event is known as the Eclipse. However, only a select few beings outside the cult are aware of their true identity and cause, one of them being the dragon [[Kharagon]].
