Since the beginning of days, there have been entire civilisations that worship everyday dragons as gods, and for good reason. They are unmatched in strength and their simple presence demands respect. To gaze upon a dragon is both an honor and a damnation to most people, and because of these polarizing emotions about dragons and their established worship, a twin pair of dragons were born. The divine brother, the Platinum dragon of justice, or more widely known as Bahamut is a god of justice and equality. He represents the idea that the strong must protect the weak and frail. His followers are encouraged to assist the people of the mortal realm, no matter what that might look like, be it humanitarian, emotional support or one's own life. Protection can only go so far when injustice can be caused by those who don't swing a blade. Those who do choose to take up arms are expected to have a shield or some way to protect those they are defending rather than focusing entirely on combat. The platinum dragon has inspired a wide range of knightly orders to spring up all around the world, some hold true to his teachings while others use their worship to justify evil, but one thing remains true. No one can hide from the divine flame of the god of justice.
