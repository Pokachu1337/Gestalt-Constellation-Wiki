They have rejected the [[Kharagon|Dragon-King]] but She has not rejected them. They have forbidden themselves from entering Her cities unless in spring. So they roam the [[Sahra|desert]] as trading nomads and mercenaries. Their ancestral cities of [[Manzil Al Ravi]] in the south and [[Manzil Al Harv]] in the north are the points between where they travel. Most of the year they travel the sand. Once in every generation, when She is in the mood of silver, the Dragon repeats Her offer, for them to rejoin Her rule. Every time they refuse and every time a few leave for the Dragon. Those who leave are treated like the dead. Honor is important, more so for artisans, wayfinders and others of civil professions, then for the warriors. If you are honorable people will request your aid and commission you. If you are not, you will only have employment for lesser tasks. Among themselves they do not trade in coin but gifts and dept. One gives a gift and honor demands you return a greater gift which will then be answered by another gift. When you can not repay a gift you pay with honor. When they arrive in Manzil Al Ravi in the south for the grand reunion all remaining debts will be repaid or forgiven. One who does not forgive a debt for 3 years will have forfeit their honor. Necromancy is one of the ancestral arts practiced by the Qamalri. Nothing can be wasted for the desert does not make prisoners. Dead pack animals are reanimated to walk the dunes again. Fallen soldiers rise again to fight once more. Artisans keep working on menial tasks. The spirits of the departed are consulted for guidance and knowledge. It is an honor and a burden to be a necromancer for the art was taught to them by the Dragon they reject. In times of war many of them serve as mercenaries for [[Schwert]], warriors, suppliers and more. Some imperial strategists highly value the Sandfolk. In times of peace they often skirmish among themselves and serve as mercenaries to local loads. In the eyes of Kahragon, the Qamalri are like rebellious children. They will come back to Her, when they are older and wiser. After all, they have only been around for two elves.
