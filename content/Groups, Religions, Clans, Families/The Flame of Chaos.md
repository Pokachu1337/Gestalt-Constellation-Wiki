Every god of justice needs its counterpart, and Tiamat, the evil goddess of dragons and destruction is the exact opposite of everything Bahamut stands for and more. Also known as the hydra goddess she takes the form of all her children, not out of respect for some level of twisted love towards her creations but as a way to manipulate and corrupt the chromatic dragons she births. This corruption can be and has been overcome before but it takes a near unshakable will and determination to break free from the will of the multi headed beast. Her worshipers seek to corrupt any dragon they come across for that is the will of their lady. They believe they'll be rewarded with salvation for their efforts and fanatical devotion to her once the age of dragons and chaos comes. They are mistaken. Some work out of desperation, some do it for revenge and others just do it to see the world burn. She cares not for why her worshipers choose her, all she cares about is bring dragons back to their true glory at the cost of reality itself
