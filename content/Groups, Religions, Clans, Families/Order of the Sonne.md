With their primary base in the mountains south of [[Krone]], the Order of the Sonne is a group of holy knights, clerics, and other religious positions, worshiping [[Vater Sonne]], the maker of the world. They are his eyes at night, and they act as his hands, delivering justice and destroying cults and heretical objects wherever they find them. Many knights are chosen when extremely young, spending their lives to one day become solders for the cause. They often clash with the [[The Enlightened of Mutter Mond|Enlightened of Mutter Mond]], perceiving them as a heretical cult, and would love nothing more than to destroy the [[Crown of the Waning Moon|crown]] they seek.
