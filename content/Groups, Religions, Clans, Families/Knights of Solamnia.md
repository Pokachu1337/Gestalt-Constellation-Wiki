Located in the [[Sahra]] desert is the Knight Order of Solamnia. While most holy orders that are associated with the [[The Platinum Fire|Platinum Fire]] focus on humanitarian works and legal support for the recovering civilizations, some know that evil will always reveal its ugly head, so instead of relying on militias and volunteer fighters, some dedicate themselves to the defense of their world from evil, no matter the reality they might come from. The knights of Solamnia are the pinnacle of these orders. They have dedicated themselves to their lord Bahamut, so much so that only those who are shown to have a natural attunement towards dragons or draconic powers are allowed into their ranks. When a young knight joins their order, they are trained under their betters until it's determined they can hold their own in combat. With their limited numbers (at most 100 members at a time) they prefer to wander alone or with any draconic allies, spreading themselves out to protect and help as many people as they possibly can. When times of war are ravaging the lands, they prefer to stay neutral and deal with any issues that might be ignored during the war, that is unless one side is clearly on the side of evil, and in that case they'll gather their forces, calling upon all holy orders that will follow their banner, and all of their draconic allies ranging from kobolds who worship their dragon allies as gods, to the mightiest and oldest dragons.
