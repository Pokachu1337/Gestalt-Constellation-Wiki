[[Reynold]] goes to finish off the [[Dread Wyvern]], finding the beast in it's layer, a cave high in the mountains. During the fight, the ground collapses to reveal a large ruin with a strange pool beneath.

_You hear behind you splashing and churning, causing you to instinctively turn around, a bad feeling washing over your entire body. Rising from the liquid of stars is a huge figure, the wyvern, but changed. It is larger, half of its skull exposed and still dripping with stars. Some parts of its body are disproportionate, one leg large and its scales dark, the other small and bright, ever changing as it comes into contact with the liquid. You see the small leg grow huge and then fall apart into bone but still animated as this colossal wyvern rises from the pond, spitting up mucus and stars. It seems sick but in a way you have never seen before and most importantly, it looks very much angry.

_The corrupted wyvern moves first, stars, blood and bile dripping on its every move as it quickly jumps at you. Able to catch its claw and redirect it, its huge mouth descends down and catches you badly. Even the starry liquid catches you, dripping from its teeth, your skin withering on contact but thankfully it doesn't hold long as you manage to gain some distance.

_After the wyvern nearly chomped him in half, he slams his sword into the abomination's leg, nearly taking it off before he's kicked away into another wall. He slams into it hard, causing rubble to fall all around him and blood to seep through the cracks of his armor. He slowly rises back up, charging forward once again, trying to slam his blade into the wyvern's bone leg, but he just wasn't fast enough to reach it in time. So he slams his blade into the monster's other leg, trying to use all my strength to knock it down distracting him for just a moment. His strength fails him as some kind of smoke fills his helmet, he starts to aggressively cough up blood as he can feel his body rotting and withering away. He falls down to his knees, trying to stand up one more time, but he just isn't strong enough._

_He crumbles down, a rotting, bloody mess. He never thought he'd die alone, Reynold always did everything he could to protect those around him, he even built himself around the idea of dying to protect others. Yet here he is, rotting away in a damp cave, all alone. Not even Adalt was by his side in his last moments, but that's not what he's thinking about. All Reynold can think about is what will happen to Adalt after he dies, he's always been by his side ever since Reynold could walk. Is Adalt going to die with him? A small tear forms in his eye as everything goes dark for one last time._

[[Reynold]] is dead. [[Adalt, the Dragon of Vengeance]] will avenge him.
