*The sun shines bright at mid day. A few travelers, merchants and pilgrims move on the lower road. As we move up the hill we get to the cloister [[Nordgraf]]. A wall about the height of two dwarfs surrounds the fields and joins the buildings of the cloister. Two shadowy figures move into the cloister through the main gate. They have learned that a patch of the [[Fenrir, the Wolf who swallows the Sun|Wolfs]] fur has been found by paladins of the [[Order of the Sonne|Order of Sonne]]. The Order is keeping it here before they move it to their citadel in the south.*

[[Vlut Bliradim, Butcher of the Eclipse]] and [[Ragna Vespera, Envoy of the Eclipse]] take a guided tour of the cloister with [[Brother August]].

In the night they murder everyone without mercy. Well not everyone, a few escape and they take Brother August prisoner. The monk shows them to the relic room and after no longer being useful Ragna murders him too.

With the relics they came for secured, they leave, the cloister behind them is in ruins.
