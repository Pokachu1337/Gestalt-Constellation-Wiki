_Since the end of the war, merchants have been traveling between the domains. Traveling over unpatrolled streets and former battlefields invites enough dangers that mercenary escorts are common, to protect from wild monsters and way laying bandits. However recent rumors speak of a necromancer, demanding tribute not in gold but bodies. This calls for better protection, than common mercenaries._

The [[Kruger Brothers]] have recruited [[Reynold]], [[Dressi, the Undeniable]] and [[Amélie Scarlatina of House Bella Muerte|Amélie]] for protection along the road. [[Anne-Laure of Chastain]] also joins them as her [[Knight of Lunéciel Mondkind|Knight]] has fallen ill.

They evade and fight off undead, ward of some owlbears and safely make the journey from [[Limivill]] to [[Grenzstett]].

The Krugers petition the Lord of Grenzstett for aid with the undead problem and tell that the village of [[Unglück]] may be infested with the undead.
